HELP US NAME IT!
A “Friends and Family” concert this Spring
We are looking forward to holding a “Friends and Family” type of concert in the Spring, but we need our own name for it – and we want you to help us out! Start thinking of a name; we will ask for ideas in the next two months. If you have a good idea right now, please feel free to send it along. This is a concert where you get together with family members and/or friends and work on a piece together to perform. At least one person in the group needs to be an OSM member.
